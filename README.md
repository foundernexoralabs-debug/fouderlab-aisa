# IronTrack AI — Smart Gym Progress, Bodybuilding & Meal Planning App

IronTrack AI is a fitness and bodybuilding progress app built for gym lovers, bodybuilders, personal trainers, and anyone serious about improving their body.

The goal is simple:

Help users train smarter, track progress clearly, eat better, and build muscle with a structured plan.

## What IronTrack AI Does

IronTrack AI helps users:

- Track workouts
- Log exercises, sets, reps, and weights
- Monitor strength progress over time
- Track body measurements
- Track body weight and body fat changes
- Create meal plans
- Track calories and macros
- Follow bodybuilding goals
- See progress charts
- Save progress photos
- Build weekly training plans
- Stay consistent with habits
- Get AI-powered improvement suggestions

## Core Features

### Workout Tracking

Users can create and track workouts for:

- Chest
- Back
- Shoulders
- Arms
- Legs
- Core
- Full body
- Push / Pull / Legs
- Upper / Lower
- Custom split routines

Each workout can include:

- Exercise name
- Sets
- Reps
- Weight
- Rest time
- Notes
- Difficulty
- Personal records

### Progress Tracking

IronTrack AI helps users understand whether they are actually improving.

Progress tracking includes:

- Strength progress
- Body weight changes
- Muscle measurements
- Progress photos
- Personal records
- Training volume
- Weekly consistency
- Body transformation timeline

### Meal Planning

Users can create meal plans based on their goal:

- Muscle gain
- Fat loss
- Maintenance
- Lean bulk
- Cutting phase
- Body recomposition

Meal planning can include:

- Calories
- Protein
- Carbs
- Fats
- Meal timing
- Grocery list
- Daily meal schedule
- Simple bodybuilding meals
- High-protein food suggestions

### AI Fitness Coach

IronTrack AI should eventually help users with:

- Workout suggestions
- Form reminders
- Recovery advice
- Meal improvement ideas
- Plateau detection
- Weak muscle group detection
- Weekly progress reviews
- Goal-based recommendations

The AI should not give dangerous medical advice. It should be clear when users should speak to a doctor, dietitian, or certified trainer.

## Main App Pages

The app should include:

1. Dashboard  
   Shows today’s workout, weekly progress, calories, weight trend, and next goal.

2. Workout Planner  
   Lets users create, edit, and follow workout routines.

3. Workout Log  
   Lets users record sets, reps, weights, notes, and personal records.

4. Progress Tracker  
   Shows charts for strength, body weight, measurements, and consistency.

5. Meal Planner  
   Helps users create meal plans and track calories/macros.

6. Body Metrics  
   Tracks weight, body fat, chest, arms, waist, legs, shoulders, and other measurements.

7. Progress Photos  
   Lets users save transformation photos over time.

8. AI Coach  
   Gives smart suggestions based on training and nutrition data.

9. Profile & Goals  
   Stores user goals, body stats, experience level, and preferences.
